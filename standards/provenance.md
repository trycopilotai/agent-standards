# Provenance

Keep a traceable relationship between source bytes,
transformations, authorship, and delivered artifacts. Record
hashes and the ordered transformations when byte identity
matters. A formatted or redacted transcript is edited; do
not describe it as raw or unedited.

Keep private provenance indexes and ingestion records in
their designated private store. Publication authorization
for a derived artifact does not automatically authorize its
private source material or the surrounding dataset.

Classify examples accurately: synthetic, public and
licensed, or private and explicitly authorized. Renaming
identifiers in private material does not make its
architecture synthetic.

Bind release claims to exact tracked surfaces and evidence.
Record absent measurements explicitly. Verify generated
tables and assets against their owning inputs. A local
receipt is an operator assertion unless an external provider
authenticates it.

Audit publication history, metadata, paths, tags, messages,
and binaries as well as the current text. Keep release
reviews bound to the exact reviewed bytes and refs; a
subsequent change requires renewed review.
